Some Musings on the Rise of Agentic Legal AI

Lawyers will revert to their stated purpose - a counselor

apr 11, 2025

Agentic Legal AI is here. And as much as you might be scared of it, it's going to be handling your emails and it's going to be responding for you. It'll be even calling certain individuals, getting information from them, and combining that into a review for a lawyer.

Lawyers are going to transition from “lawyer as the loop” to “lawyer in the loop.” They'll simply review the outputs of AI agents. Think of every single administrative non-billable task that you do as a lawyer. That all can be abstracted away. And every day, you, as a lawyer will come to the office and rubber stamp exactly what you think is good and what you think isn't.

The value add that you'll have as a lawyer won't be in how close of a reading you can do of a paper or your vast knowledge of legal corpus and case texts, but it'll be your judgment. This is really what being a lawyer is. It's the counselor. It's someone who understands, given a certain situation, what are the best options given your goals. And so lawyers as counselors will come back, and I'm excited for this new revolution.

In the coming days, weeks and months, I'll explore some of the uses of agentic AI and how lawyers can work in symbiosis with them.
