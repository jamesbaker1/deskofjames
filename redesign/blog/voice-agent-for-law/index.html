Voice Agent for Law

Is voice the ideal method to interact with AI tools?

apr 19, 2025

Hey, everyone.

Today I want to talk about communications management. The legal technology sector is lacking in this area. Think of it this way: you have a central management system such as Clio or another system or record that handles all of your clients and their interactions. But leveraging value out of these interactions and connecting them when you need them the most, like when a client is talking to you, is not yet a solved problem.

With the recent advancements in voice agents, I'm now starting to think that the cleanest human-computer interaction with artificial intelligence systems is through voice. These voice interactions, paired with an agentic structure that can act on your behalf, may in fact be the highest leverage that you can get out of these systems with the lowest amount of integration costs.

I'm super excited to explore this space. If you want to reach out specifically about this area, shoot me an email.
